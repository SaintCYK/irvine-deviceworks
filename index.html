What we repair

Every repair starts with an assessment and a quote. You approve the price before any work begins.

  • PlayStation 5 and 4HDMI ports, overheating, SSD upgrades and fault diagnosis.
  • Xbox One, Series X and SHDMI ports, overheating and fault diagnosis.
  • Nintendo SwitchScreen replacement, game card reader, Joy-Con and joystick repairs.
  • Controllers and stick driftPlayStation, Xbox and Switch. Joystick replacement, Hall-effect upgrades, buttons and shells.
  • iPhone and AndroidScreens, batteries, charging ports, cameras, speakers and microphones.
  • TabletsiPad, Android and Amazon Fire. Screens, batteries and charging ports.
  • Laptops, desktops and MacsScreens, batteries, cooling, Windows and macOS fixes, iMac repairs.
  • PC builds and upgradesCustom and gaming PCs, SSD, RAM, CPU and graphics card upgrades.
  • Skins and accessoriesConsole and controller skins, HDMI and power cables. Ordered items can take around two weeks.

We don't repair TVs, drones or keyboards, and we don't offer data recovery. All repairs depend on assessment and parts availability.

How it works

Repairs are done at our own workshop. The shop is only a drop-off and collection point.

Questions people ask

How does home pickup work?

Message us on WhatsApp and describe the fault. If it looks fixable, we arrange a pickup from your home, repair the device and bring it back. Pickup and return is a flat £30.

Do I pay before you start?

No. We send a quote first and only start once you approve it.

What if you can't fix it?

You don't pay for the repair, and the £30 pickup and return fee is waived.

What if I change my mind after pickup?

We only book a pickup once you've agreed and we've told you the device looks fixable. If you then decline the repair, the £30 pickup and return fee still applies.

I dropped it off at the shop. Can you deliver it to me?

Yes. Delivery to your home after a shop drop-off is a flat £15. Collecting from the shop is free.

Are repairs done in the shop?

No. The shop is a drop-off and collection point only. Repairs are done at our own workshop.

I can't use WhatsApp. What now?

Visit the drop-off shop and use the iPad there to message us.

Broken device? We'll collect it.

Message us what's wrong. Quote first, and we only collect once we know it's fixable.
